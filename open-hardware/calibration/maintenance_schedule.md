# Maintenance Schedule

| Interval | Task | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly during first month | Inspect data freshness and battery voltage | Confirms installation stability. |
| Monthly | Clean rain gauge, inspect enclosure seals and cable glands | Remove dust, insects and plant debris. |
| Monthly | Check solar panel surface and mounting | Clean safely if output is degraded. |
| Monthly | Review missing-data and out-of-range alerts | Investigate stale or drifting sensors. |
| Seasonal | Recheck soil probe placement and cable strain relief | Reinstall if soil movement exposes probes. |
| Seasonal | Verify wind vane alignment and anemometer rotation | Especially after storms. |
| Annual | Review calibration coefficients and battery health | Replace degraded batteries safely. |

Record maintenance with generic node IDs and avoid publishing private location or operator details.
