# Weather Station Calibration

Weather station calibration should be documented before operational use.

## Air Temperature and Humidity

- Compare against a reference instrument in shade.
- Use a radiation shield and avoid direct heat sources.
- Record sensor offset if persistent bias is observed.

## Rain Gauge

- Level the gauge.
- Verify tip count with a measured water volume.
- Record `rain_mm_per_tip`.
- Inspect for debris after windy or dusty periods.

## Wind Sensors

- Check anemometer free rotation.
- Record wind vane north alignment offset.
- Compare wind speed against a nearby reference where feasible.

## Solar Radiation

- Ensure horizontal mounting.
- Avoid shade, bird droppings and reflective surfaces.
- Record whether the sensor is calibrated pyranometer class or relative irradiance sensor.

## Pressure

- Compare with a local reference and document altitude correction if used.
