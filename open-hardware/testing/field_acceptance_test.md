# Field Acceptance Test

Use this checklist after installation:

- Node enclosure is secure and weatherproof.
- Cable glands are sealed and strain relief is installed.
- Soil probes are installed at planned depth and have stable readings.
- Weather station is level and free of obvious obstructions.
- Solar panel is secure and not shaded during expected charging window.
- Battery voltage is within normal range.
- Telemetry arrives at edge gateway or cloud ingestion.
- Payload validates against schema.
- Data freshness is visible in AgriTwin.
- Node identifiers are generic and do not reveal private infrastructure labels.
- Maintenance log entry is created without private operator details.
