# Electrical and Field Safety

## Batteries and Solar Charging

- Use protected battery packs and chemistry-matched chargers.
- Do not use damaged or swollen batteries.
- Provide fusing and over-current protection where appropriate.
- Keep batteries away from excessive heat.
- Follow local regulations for battery transport and disposal.

## Weatherproofing

- Use IP-rated enclosures and cable glands.
- Provide drip loops.
- Seal unused openings.
- Inspect after storms or high wind.

## Cable and Machinery Safety

- Bury or protect cables where machinery may pass.
- Mark installations clearly for field operators.
- Avoid trip hazards.
- Use strain relief on all external cables.

## Lightning and Surge Considerations

- Outdoor masts and long cable runs may require surge protection and qualified installation.
- Follow local electrical standards.
- Disconnect or isolate equipment before service where required.

## Maintenance Safety

- Schedule maintenance outside hazardous field operations.
- Use appropriate personal protective equipment.
- Avoid working on powered outdoor equipment during storms.
