# Field Installation Guidance

## Sensor Placement

- Place soil probes in representative crop zones.
- Avoid exact private coordinates in public documents.
- Record depth, crop context and general position class in local deployment metadata.
- Protect cables from machinery, animals and UV exposure.

## Weather Station Placement

- Avoid shade and local obstructions.
- Keep rain gauge level.
- Mount wind sensors at a representative height where feasible.
- Keep solar radiation sensor clear of shadows.

## Edge Gateway Placement

- Prefer a secure, dry, serviceable location.
- Ensure stable power and network access.
- Provide ventilation and surge protection.
- Keep local credentials and service configuration outside git.
