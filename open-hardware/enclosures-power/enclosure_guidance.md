# Enclosure Guidance

## Recommended Features

- IP65 or IP67 weather rating.
- UV-stable material.
- Cable glands matched to cable diameter.
- Internal mounting plate.
- Drip loops on external cables.
- Desiccant or venting where condensation is expected.
- Label with generic node ID and maintenance contact.

## Layout

- Keep battery separated from heat-generating electronics.
- Place antenna outside metal enclosures or use an external antenna connector.
- Avoid mounting electronics below cable entry points.
- Leave enough service space for battery replacement and USB access.

## Field Notes

- Do not place enclosures where farm machinery can strike them.
- Avoid standing water and irrigation spray.
- Ensure mounting hardware resists corrosion.
