# Code of Conduct

AgriTwin contributors are expected to keep collaboration professional, respectful, and focused on safe agricultural digital-twin software.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and respectful language.
- Be direct about technical risks without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and data-protection constraints.
- Avoid publishing sensitive farm, personal, network, or credential information.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal insults.
- Publishing private data, credentials, or operational details.
- Deliberately bypassing security, privacy, or licence controls.
- Misrepresenting synthetic demo data as validated pilot evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behaviour puts people, farms, project partners, or repository safety at risk.
